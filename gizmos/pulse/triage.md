# TRIAGE — Pulse

Why this gizmo, against the dictate:

The dictate asks for the first *connectable* gizmo — the 165 already in the
catalogue are fields with no one to talk to. The smallest thing that proves
connection is a float generator: one output, no inputs, driven only by `t`.
A slow sine. It is the LFO of every synthesizer and the tick gizmo the
addendum asked for, in one.

Because: a pulse is the simplest signal that is alive. Anything it is wired
to starts breathing.

Kills: static, screensaver. If it cannot be plugged in, it is neither of
those problems solved.
